Our Story

A little garden that
happens to make coffee.

Brew & Bloom started in 2019 as a six-seat coffee bar inside a florist's shop on 12th Main. Today we roast our own beans, bake everything from scratch and still fill every table with flowers from local growers in Hosur.

Meera & Arjun RaoFounders & head roasters
